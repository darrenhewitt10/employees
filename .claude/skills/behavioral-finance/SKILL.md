# Behavioral finance

## Biases to watch
Loss aversion and panic selling · revenge trading after losses · overconfidence after wins · recency bias (extrapolating the last month) · confirmation bias in research · sunk cost (keeping a failed strategy) · action bias (overtrading - the main failure in AI trading contests) · FOMO on hyped assets.

## Countermeasures
- **Pre-commitment:** kill criteria and limits are written before going live.
- **Cooling-off:** any owner request to loosen a limit or override the system waits >= 24 hours and is logged with a reason.
- **Checklists** for overrides: what changed in the evidence, not in the P&L?
- **Outcome vs process:** judge decisions by process quality; review losing and winning periods alike.
- **Reporting design:** show drawdowns and benchmark alongside returns; avoid intraday P&L notifications except for alerts.

## Output
Name the bias you see, the evidence for it, and the specific counter-step.
