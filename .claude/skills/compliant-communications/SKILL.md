# Compliant communications

## Rules
- Never state or imply guaranteed or "can't lose" returns; regulators flag this as a fraud signal.
- Show performance **net of fees and costs**, with the benchmark, max drawdown and the full period - no cherry-picked windows.
- Label backtested/hypothetical/paper results clearly as such, with key assumptions.
- Say what AI does and doesn't do here (features and oversight, not autonomous trading).
- Include a plain-language risk statement and "not investment advice".
- No vendor data or client data in public material.

## Registration trigger
Publishing signals, performance for marketing, or offering the system to others can require investment-adviser or CTA registration. **Check with securities-regulatory-counsel before anything goes public.**

## Review flow
Draft -> performance-analyst checks every number -> counsel review -> owner approval.
