# Decision memo

Use this template. Keep it to one page unless asked for more.

```markdown
## Decision: <question in one line>
**Recommendation:** <option> - <one-sentence why>
**Decision owner:** owner | <agent>   **Needed by:** <date/phase>

### Options
| Option | Pros | Cons | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Why this option
- Evidence (cite docs/ and sources)

### Risks and mitigations
- ...

### What would change the recommendation
- Specific facts or thresholds

### Vetoes / sign-offs required
- risk-manager? validation-statistician? owner? counsel?
```

## Rules
- Always include a **do nothing / passive benchmark** option where relevant.
- Quantify where possible (cost per month, expected drawdown, hours of work).
- Separate facts (cited) from judgment (labelled).
- If the decision loosens a limit or adds risk, state that **owner approval is required**.
