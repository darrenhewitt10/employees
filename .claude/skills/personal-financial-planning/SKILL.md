# Personal financial planning (research, not personalized financial advice)

## Before any capital is committed
- Emergency fund in place; high-interest debt paid down.
- Time horizon and liquidity needs for the money.
- Only **risk capital** - money the owner can lose entirely without changing their life - goes into automated active strategies.
- Core long-term savings stay in diversified, low-cost passive investments.

## Account choice
- Taxable cash account (default): flexible, tax drag on turnover.
- IRA: tax-free trading inside, limited instruments, contribution limits; wash sales across taxable/IRA still apply.
- Margin: disabled by default.

## Questions for the owner (doc 12)
Capital amount and loss tolerance · objective · max drawdown · time to monitor · existing accounts · tax professional involvement.

## Output
A recommended allocation: core passive vs automated sleeve %, starting live capital (small fraction of planned), and conditions for adding more.
